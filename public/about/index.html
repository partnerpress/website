The short version

I discovered WordPress in 2010 and was hooked. Since then I've helped hundreds of businesses, from solo practitioners to brands like Sutter Health and Grifols, get websites that are stable, secure and easy to find.

Most of my clients come to me after a developer disappeared or a site broke at the worst possible time. My job is to take that off your plate, so you can focus on running your business while I handle the website, the rankings and the ads.

I split my time between Novato, CA and Las Cruces, NM. I'm a husband, a proud stepfather to two boys, and the dutiful servant of three scrappy lap dogs. When I'm not at my desk, you'll probably find me on a bike on a back road.
